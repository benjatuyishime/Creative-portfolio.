02 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORK

Rooted in culture.
Driven by imagination.

I'm Benjamin Tuyishime, a Rwandan digital artist, illustrator and car content creator based in Kigali. Passionate about African storytelling, I create visually engaging illustrations, animations and branded content inspired by African culture, landscapes and contemporary youth creativity.

My practice spans animation development, visual storytelling and social media content, with a focus on projects that bring art, education and social impact together. I've contributed to awareness-driven creative initiatives exploring climate solutions and challenges such as deforestation, poaching and water pollution through child-centered storytelling.

Alongside my artistic practice, I'm active in automotive media and culture, blending cinematic visuals and design-led content to create engaging experiences around cars and creativity.
